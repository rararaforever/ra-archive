How To Use
THE SEARCH BAR

The search bar searches the title of an item as well as its description and tags. This is a good place to look up via keywords, or if you have something specific you are looking for. It searches live as you type, so sometimes there may not be anything tagged exactly for what you are looking for (ie. “Thailand”) but there are entries for something similar (ie. “Thai”). Consider different terms and keywords to use to find what you are searching for.

DESKTOP ONLY ↓
HOT KEYS

ENTER will automatically search what you have typed in the search bar

ESC will automatically minimize the full screen search page

FILTERS

These filters are a great place to start exploring. These filters are divided into 6 sections:

  • Medium (What type of format is the entry about? Short film, feature film, Event)
  • Programme (Explore Reel Asian’s different programming subsections like: Community Archive Submission, RA:X, Reel Ideas)
  • Tags (There are over 100 tags in this database, they range from topic, genre, description of event and more)
  • Year (Filter by a specific festival year)
  • Language (Language of video or text based piece)
  • Country (Country of film production)
VIEWING OPTIONS

The bottom right of the page features different options to browse the archive.

The slider controls the number of columns you can view the results in.

Grid view: Provides an image forward view of the archive, in a square grid format

List view: Provides a more detailed view of the entries through a wider view. This is recommended for more research based browsing

Randomizer: Click and find out!

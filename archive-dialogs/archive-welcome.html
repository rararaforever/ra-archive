Welcome to the Reel Asian Digital Community Archive!

This is a community archive, please navigate with sensitivity and mindfulness. Users must adhere to the unique copyright and sharing permissions on each of the entries. Sharing of material against the wishes of the copyright holder’s discretion is strictly prohibited.

This is a living archive, therefore not a complete directory of all films and events from Reel Asian, but rather pieces of it. We focused on items submitted from community members, films already available online and some things in our own archive to release.

If you just want to explore: Browse using the filtering options, and see what you find!

Or, type in a key word, place or name in the search bar.

Here you will find:

  • Memorabilia and keepsakes of community members at Reel Asian over the years
  • Several commissioned and programmed films available online, searchable via tags, names and key words
  • Archived panels and talks hosted through our industry series
  • Archived Internal, Operational and Marketing Reel Asian documents and items
  • Critical writing on programmed films/panels: Youth Critics Initiative Pieces (TACLA)
  • Wee Asian arts activities over the last 5 years
  • And more!

If there is an entry that involves you or your likeness in the archive that you want removed or edited, please reach out to specialprojects@reelasian.com to have it addressed.

Wander around, and have fun!
